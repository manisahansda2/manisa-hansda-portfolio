About

Building inclusive, evidence-based social impact

I am a social development professional with a Master's in Social Work from TISS Mumbai, focused on livelihoods, monitoring & evaluation, and community-centred programme design.

My work spans participatory research, capacity building, government stakeholder liaison, and translating field data into actionable insights for donors and leadership teams.
